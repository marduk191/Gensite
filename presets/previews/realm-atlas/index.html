Ages of Veyra

  1. Age of Glass · Sample year 0–400. The first towns grow around lakes that never thaw.
  2. Age of Gilt · 401–900. Trade roads join the coasts; the Cartographers’ Table is founded.
  3. The Drowning · 901. The eastern lowlands slip beneath the tide and become the Sunken Marches.
  4. Age of Embers · 902 to the present. The beacons are lit and the long watch begins.
How to adapt this page
