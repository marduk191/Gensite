Model summary

Larkspur-7B is a decoder-only transformer fine-tuned for summarising, rewriting and answering questions over supplied documents. It prefers to quote its source and to say when it does not know.

Quick start

from sampletoolkit import load, generate

model = load("fieldnote-labs/larkspur-7b", dtype="4bit")
print(generate(model, "Summarise the attached notes in three bullets.",
               documents=["notes.txt"], max_tokens=256))
sample-run larkspur-7b --doc notes.txt --prompt "Summarise in three bullets" --max-tokens 256

Training and evaluation

Data

  • 2.1T tokens of licensed and public-domain text (demo figure)
  • Instruction mix of 410k reviewed examples
  • Dataset card: see FAQ

Compute

  • 64 accelerators for 11 days (demo)
  • Estimated 2.4 t CO2e, reported as a sample
  • Evaluated with a fixed harness, three seeds

Limitations and intended use

Intended

  • Drafting and summarising supplied documents
  • Research assistants with a human reviewer
  • Local, offline text tools

Not intended

  • Legal, medical or financial decisions without review
  • Unattended publication of generated claims
  • Languages outside the three listed

It can state false details with confidence, may reflect biases in its training text, and degrades past about 6k tokens of context. Always check quotes against the source.
