Tips

Are these prompts tested?

They are demo text for a template. Test and date your own prompts before publishing a real library.

Does copying send anything anywhere?

No. The button only copies text on your device; this page is static.

Why show blanks in colour?

So a reader never sends a prompt with a placeholder still in it.
