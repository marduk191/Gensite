Quick key

Which habitat are you in?
  1. Standing water or wet margin: go to the Pond plates.
  2. Dry, open and springy underfoot: go to the Heath plates.
  3. Thorny, linear and full of birdsong: go to the Hedgerow plates.
How are plates numbered?

Plates run in order of first entry in the notebook, not by family. Replace with your own scheme.

What do the status words mean?

Abundant, common, frequent, occasional and local are demo labels for how often a species is met on the common.

Can I add my own species?

Yes. Copy a plate figure, change the number, name, Latin binomial and drawing.
