Hearth-Ward

Ward · Rank 2 · Ritual

Chalk a circle at dusk; no cold nor uninvited whisper crosses it until the first light.

Lantern Bolt

Flame · Rank 1 · Instant

A thread of amber light leaps from your wick-hand and stings whatever it strikes.

Mile-Folding

Wandering · Rank 3 · Minute

Fold the road like cloth; one stride crosses a league, but the road remembers the debt.

Watcher’s Veil

Ward · Rank 1 · Hour

Dim glass forms before your eyes, showing the hidden shape of any lock or snare.

Cinder Bloom

Flame · Rank 4 · Instant

A seed of ember opens into a ring of falling sparks. Friends are spared; shadows are not.

Moth-Step

Wandering · Rank 1 · Round

Weigh nothing for one breath, and cross water, ash or rotten floorboards unmarked.

Campaign codex

Notes the circle keeps between sessions. Open an entry to read it.

The Cinder Road

A long causeway of black glass across the Ashfen. Lamps stand every mile; keeping them lit is the circle’s oldest oath.

The Lamplighters

A guild of wanderers, half scholars and half couriers. Each carries one lantern and one secret they have promised not to read aloud.

Tavern of Low Tide

The circle’s safe house. Its landlord, Mother Orrel, is a fictional character for this demo and keeps a ledger of every favour owed.

Session 7 · The Drowned Archive

The party found a flooded vault of unlit lamps. One answered to a name nobody had spoken. Return to the spell index.
