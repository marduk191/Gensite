Tidepool etiquette

Look, don't lift

Wet your hands first, move slowly and put every stone back the way it was.

Watch the water

Turn back before the rising tide cuts off your route. Check the real forecast.

What to carry

Grippy boots, a hand lens, a notebook and a waterproof pencil.
