Why rank by pairs?

People are better at choosing between two answers than at assigning a score to one. Pairwise votes become a rating, and the interval shows how sure the sample is.

Overlapping intervals mean “too close to call”, so the table avoids implying a gap that the data cannot support.

Methodology notes

  1. Blind pairs

    Voters see two anonymous answers to the same prompt; model names appear only after the vote.

  2. Rating model

    Votes are fitted with a Bradley–Terry style model and bootstrapped for intervals. Replace this with your real method.

  3. Contamination checks

    Prompts are filtered against known training sets. State your own policy here.

  4. Refresh cadence

    Sample cadence: weekly. A real board should print a visible “last updated” date.

FAQ

Are these real models?

No. Names, ratings and vote counts are fictional placeholders for layout reference.

Can I submit a model?

This template links nowhere. A real board should link to a submission guide.

Why show confidence intervals?

A rank without uncertainty implies precision the sample may not have.
